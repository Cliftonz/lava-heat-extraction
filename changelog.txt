Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-09-06
  Info:
    - Initial release of this fork of Vulcanus Lava Power by Orbital917, renamed to Lava Heat Extraction.
    - Every prototype and setting this mod defines is namespaced under lhe-, so it can be enabled alongside vulcanus-lava-power.
  Features:
    - Added the lhe-filtered-lava fluid and a lava sluicing recipe that turns 500 lava into 240 filtered lava and 30 stone in a foundry over 4 seconds.
    - Lava sluicing uses the metallurgy category and is unlocked by the Tungsten carbide technology.
    - The filtered lava heating tower burns lhe-filtered-lava instead of raw lava.
  Changes:
    - Added the startup setting lhe-sluicing-output for filtered lava produced per sluicing craft.
    - Added the startup setting lhe-sluicing-stone for stone produced per sluicing craft; set it to 0 to disable the stone byproduct.
